EMGMS ADMISSIONS REQUIREMENTS

Master of Science in Engineering Management Application(EMGMS) Checklist
Application Materials

 Transcripts from previous institutions
Must show completion of a Bachelor’s degree. (minimum 3.00 out of 4.00)
Must be in original native language AND be accompanied by officially verified English translation.

 Statement of Purpose (1-2 pages)
What are your goals and objectives for pursuing this graduate degree? What are your qualifications and indicators of success in this endeavor? Include career objectives that obtaining this degree will provide.

 Letters of Recommendation (Minimum 3)
Letters should be written by faculty or employers that can speak about your academic and research ability and your potential for success in graduate school.
Must be written on university/company letterhead and signed by the recommender.

 CV/Resume
May include education, honors, memberships and participation in professional organizations, employment history, research and scholarly activities and publications.

 English Proficiency Scores (all test scores must be dated within 2 years of enrollment term)
To satisfy the English proficiency requirement you must submit one of the following:
TOEFL – minimum of 79 iBT (or 60 on the revised PBT with no section score lower than 15).
IELTS – minimum composite score of 7, with no subject area below a 6
Pearson PTE Academic – minimum of 60
Graduate English Language Endorsement from UA Center for English as Second Language (CESL)
