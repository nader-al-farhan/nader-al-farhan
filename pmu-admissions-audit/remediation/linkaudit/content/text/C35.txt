TEST OF ENGLISH AS A FOREIGN LANGUAGE

TOEFL iBT

Applicants can bypass the English courses in the Preparatory Program or alternatively might be admitted to different level in the Preparatory Program if the candidate provides a valid TOEFL iBT score report and successfully meets one of the below minimum requirements of the categories below:

LEVEL	TOEFL iBT Overall	TOEFL iBT Writing
Core	83	19
Advanced	63	15
Intermediate	54	13
Beginner	42	11
Pre-Beginner	N/A	N/A
The validation of the TOEFL iBT report	Two years from the date of TOEFL iBT test
to
the commencement date of the applied semester

(online or home edition is not accepted)
The original TOEFL iBT report has to be sent directly from ETS to PMU
