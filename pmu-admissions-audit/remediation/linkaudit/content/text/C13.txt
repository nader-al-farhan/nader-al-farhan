MBA ADMISSIONS REQUIREMENTS

Admissions to the Master of Business Administration (MBA) program

The Master of Business Administration (MBA) is a 36 credit-hour evening program that can be pursued on a full-time or part-time basis, thus allowing participants full flexibility when integrating graduate studies into their demanding personal and professional lives. After fulfilling common MBA core requirements, participants can choose from six different concentrations: General Management, International Finance, Project Management, Entrepreneurship and Innovation, Supply Chain Management, and Marketing. Admission into the MBA does not require an undergraduate business degree, but non-business majors may be given the opportunity to take four business foundation courses before beginning their MBA coursework which can then be completed in as few as two years. The program intends to empower participants to successfully launch their own enterprises or to excel in middle and upper management positions at companies of all sizes and in all industries.

Admission Criteria

Educational Background:
A Bachelor’s Degree from a recognized university
A minimum GPA of 2.50 out of 4.00 or equivalent (Very Good) in Bachelor’s Degree

English Language Proficiency Test:
A valid IELTS certificate (Academic version) with minimum scores of 6.0 overall and 5.5 in each skill or equivalent TOEFL iBT minimum scores of 83 overall and 19 in each skill (online or home edition is not accepted)

Personal Interview:
Candidates will be personally interviewed by committee of program administrators and faculty
