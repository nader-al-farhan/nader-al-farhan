## 4. Email text (English — submission-ready)

**Subject:** RE: comments on Admission pages

Dear Mr. Radhi,

Further to my email of 26 October 2025 in this thread, and as requested, I have completed a renewed, evidence-based review of the Admissions Office webpage. The review covered the five sections listed on the page (Future Students, International Students' Office, Graduate Admissions, Undergraduate Admissions and Undergraduate Placement Tests), their 35 direct destinations, the two linked PDF guides, and the official fee and programme pages used to verify specific statements. The baseline reading was carried out on 10–11 October 2026, and all findings were re-checked against the live website on 21 October 2026. The review was read-only: no website content was changed and no applicant system was accessed.

**Principal findings**

The review records 131 findings: 4 of the highest priority (P0), 21 high (P1), 50 moderate (P2) and 56 minor (P3). These include 14 proven published conflicts. It also documents eight evidenced strengths, notably the structured publication of requirements by applicant category and an official fee schedule that states its VAT basis. The issues of highest priority are:

1. **Admission cycle.** The only published admission calendar covers Fall 2026-2027, and all of its dates have passed. The application portal states that no events are currently published, and the footer link "Academic Calendar" opens the 2021/2022 calendar.
2. **Application fees.** The International Students page states SAR 500 (excl. VAT) for international transfer and visiting applicants, whereas the Transfer and Visiting pages and the official fees page state SAR 950 (incl. VAT). The College of Medicine page states SAR 1,000, whereas the Medical Program fee schedule states SAR 1,150.
3. **Admission requirements.** The International Students page still weights SAT II at 30%, although the test has been discontinued. The IELTS minimums for several graduate programmes differ between their requirement pages and their application forms (6.0 versus 6.5), and the Engineering Management requirements page contains text referring to another university's language centre.

Where pages conflict, the review does not select a value. Each conflict is recorded as a proven published conflict that requires institutional confirmation. Of the six amendments requested in this thread on 26 October 2025, three are not yet reflected and three are partially reflected (Annex A of the report).

**Attachments**

1. *01_PMU_Admissions_Content_Review_Report_EN.pdf*: the management report, covering findings, evidence, recommendations, the decisions requested and a proposed follow-up plan.
2. *02_PMU_Admissions_Content_Review_Register_EN.xlsx*: the full register, covering resource inventory, findings, evidence, coverage, the Arabic–English comparison, decisions, the follow-up plan, quality control, and 48 ready editorial corrections that do not alter any value or requirement.
3. Arabic versions of both documents (*03_…_AR.pdf* and *04_…_AR.xlsx*). [Delete this item if not attached.]

**Actions requested**

1. That you kindly prepare, or direct the preparation and completion of, the institutional management package on the basis of the attached report and register.
2. That you review the ten decisions set out in the report (D-01 to D-10) and confirm the responsible owners and follow-up arrangements. The most time-sensitive are application fees and their VAT basis (D-01), the current admission cycle (D-02), and the undergraduate and graduate eligibility and English-language requirements (D-03 and D-04).
3. That you approve the 48 editorial corrections for implementation (D-09).

To complete the assessment of the matters that remain unresolved, the following institutional information would be required:

- the approved, dated fee schedule by applicant category, including the VAT basis;
- the dates and channels of the current or next admission cycle;
- the approved undergraduate and graduate admission criteria, including the minimum weighted totals and English-language thresholds;
- the authoritative list of programmes currently open for admission;
- the designated content owner for each page;
- any aggregate enquiry or web-analytics data that may be used.

The package is therefore presented for management review and is not a closure certificate (status: NOT READY FOR VERIFIED CLOSURE).
