# Findings Register — PMU Admissions (independent audit)

**Verification date for every finding:** 2026-09-27 (F-01–F-30) · 2026-09-28 (F-31–F-45)
**Evidence method:** live page text extracted from the accessibility tree through the user's Opera browser (verbatim quotes below), plus viewport screenshots in `../evidence/screenshots/`. Screenshots capture the top of each page only, because the connector cannot scroll. Where a screenshot does not show the quoted line itself, the "Screenshot proves" column says exactly what it does show. External facts are in `../evidence/external-verification.md` (X01–X08).
**Live re-verification (2026-09-27, 19:09–19:20 UTC, audit container):** the G3 verifier re-fetched all 21 core surfaces (`../remediation/verification/report-live-2026-09-27.md`) and a read-only Playwright/Chromium probe measured them at 390×844 and 1366×768 (`../evidence/dom-probes-2026-09-27.md`, full-page screenshots S30–S35). No finding was overturned. One detail of F-02 was corrected (see self-correction log). F-29 and F-30 are new.
**Severity scale:** Critical = can change an eligibility or admission decision, money owed or the application route, with no reliable way for the applicant to tell which value is right. High = materially misleading or blocking on a decision path. Medium = friction, inconsistency or governance gap with indirect decision impact. Low = quality or polish. Enhancement = improvement beyond a defect.
**Status vocabulary:** *Verified Conflict* = two or more PMU-published values for the same rule. *Verified Error* = contradicted by an authoritative external source. *Verified Gap* = required information is absent. *Owner Decision Required* = the audit does not choose the correct value.
