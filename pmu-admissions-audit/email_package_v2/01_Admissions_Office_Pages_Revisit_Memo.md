# 1. Purpose
Follow-up to the thread "comments on Admission pages" (October 2025 – September 2026), as requested, to revisit the Admissions Office pages and identify comments of the same kind and any others. This review covers **only the content published on the website**.

# 2. What was reviewed
- The Admissions Office page, its 37 directly linked pages and the application portal welcome page (39 units). The Contact Us staff list was not copied because it contains personal names.
- Every page was read line by line on a mobile view (390 px), including tables and content inside tabs. The `/apply` page was read from its source because it redirects visitors immediately.
- Each item previously requested in the thread was re-checked against the live page.
- Read-only. No page, form or payment was changed or submitted. The desktop view was not tested.

# 4. Priority 1 items
1. **International CS/Engineering criteria** still require SAT II, which has been discontinued since 2021.
2. **International transfer/visiting fee:** shown as SAR 500 (excl. VAT); the transfer and visiting pages show SAR 950 (incl. VAT).
3. **Medicine application fee:** SAR 1,000 on the admission page vs SAR 1,150 on the medical fees page.
4. **Admission calendar:** covers Fall 2026 only and all its dates have passed. The application portal shows "No events are currently published".
5. **EMGMS page** contains another university's text ("UA Center for English as Second Language (CESL)"). Its English requirements differ from all other graduate pages, and it has no application link.
6. **Non-Saudi applicants:** the route and deadline are not stated on the international page, and the calendar shows the route as "Closed" with no date.
