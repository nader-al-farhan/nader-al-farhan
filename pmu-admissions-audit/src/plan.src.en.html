Executive summary

Executive Summary
72live pages and resources read in full
30documented findings
4critical: eligibility, money, refunds and the application route
11confirmed conflicts between university pages, plus two contradictions within a single page
4different weighting schemes for undergraduate admission
0rule pages showing an owner or an effective date

Bottom line: The audit covered 72 live resources, including every graduate, fees and refund page. It was then re-verified live on the same day: the G3 verifier found all 21 of its issues unchanged on 21 core surfaces, the university had fixed none of them, and a full review re-matched every quoted line in the registers against the live text. The problem is not a set of isolated errors. The root cause, proven by the evidence, is that there is no single source of truth for admission rules, fees and dates. Each rule is copied by hand onto several pages from different content generations, with no owner and no effective date. So applicants see contradictory values for the same decision.

  • Eligibility: Four different weighting schemes. For the general high-school applicant alone, two conflicting formulas are published, 60/40 and 30/50/20 with an interview, plus a third legacy rule that is still published (P20: an 80% threshold, or 70% with an interview and a battery test). English thresholds also conflict: IELTS 5.5 vs 6.0, and TOEFL 65 vs 83. The SAT equivalent is stated three different ways.
  • Money: The Medicine application fee is SAR 1,000 on the admission page and SAR 1,150 on the fees page. The SAR 20,000 non-refundable seat reservation is not mentioned on the admission page. The payment policy says “All tuition payments are non-refundable” and then publishes a refund table on the same page.
  • Medicine programme: Its duration is “7-year hybrid model” on the admission page and “6-year integrated curriculum” on the college page. The composite minimum (86) cannot be reached at the published component minimums, which give 83.
  • Copied and outdated content: The Engineering Management master's requirements page contains an English-language route that belongs to the University of Arizona. The “Web Admission Guide”, linked from every admissions page, is dated 2018 and describes a retired system.
  • Application route: The admissions calendar sends Saudi applicants to the Qabool platform and non-Saudis to Study in Saudi, while six other pages send everyone to the university portal. The portal itself shows “No events are currently published”.
  • Language and accessibility: No Arabic version of the binding admission rules was found, and no admissions page on pmu.edu.sa declares its language (the lang attribute is missing on 20 of 20). On the positive side, no page overflows horizontally on mobile. Arabic appears only in the direct-entry block of the apply page and in the query-form introduction.

Recommendation: approve an executive project of 6 workstreams over 22 weeks from approval, ending before the Fall 2027 admission cycle opens. The opening date is an assumption based on the Fall 2026 cycle having opened on 2026-05-03. The decisive item on the critical path is ten institutional decisions (section 12); no technical team can correct the content before they are made.

03

Why management action is required now

Why Management Action Is Required

Impact on the university

  • Defensibility of admission decisions: when the university publishes conflicting criteria, any rejection or acceptance becomes open to challenge.
  • Financial commitments: collecting an amount different from the one announced, and not disclosing a SAR 20,000 non-refundable fee in advance.
  • Alignment with national platforms: the university states the Qabool and Study in Saudi routes, then shows another route that bypasses them.
  • Reputation: unedited draft text, “launch phase” wording and spelling errors on published policy pages.

Why the web team alone is not enough

Most of the 11 conflicts require deciding the correct value, which is an academic or financial decision, not an editorial one. We did not resolve any conflict ourselves. Every conflict without a decisive official source is classified in this plan as Owner Decision Required.

Without a governance model, contradictions will return with the next fee or threshold update. This has already happened: three generations of undergraduate fee tables are shown side by side (2026/2027 intake, 2025/2026 intake, and continuing students 2024–2025).

Frame of reference: transparency and digital transformation of services under Saudi Vision 2030, and the W3C WCAG 2.2 accessibility standard. The frame was used as guiding context only; we did not measure compliance with either.

05

Target state

Target State · Admissions Canonical Data

Every rule, number and date is stored once in an admissions canonical record, each with an owner, an effective date and Arabic and English labels. Every surface (the website in both languages, the portal, the calendar, the files) shows the value from that same record; hand-typing values into pages is not allowed.

Target-state principles

  • One value per rule, per applicant category, per admission cycle.
  • Every rule component shows: “Effective for intake X · Owner · Last verified”.
  • Arabic and English come from the same value; no manual translation of numbers.
  • “Apply now” is a routing step, not one link for every applicant.
  • No legacy page is deleted before a replacement, a redirect and verification exist.

Canonical data domains (14)

GPA threshold · Qudrat threshold · Tahseely rule and equivalents · weighted formula and minimum · English tests and thresholds (with the TOEFL 1–6 scale) · test validity · application fees by programme · placement-test fee · seat reservation and refund terms · tuition by college and cycle · VAT statement · application channel by category · documents by category · calendar, intake status and contacts.

06

Project scope

Project Objective · Scope · Out of Scope

Project objective: every applicant, in Arabic or English, finds one correct, approved and dated value for every rule that affects their eligibility, cost, deadline and application channel, on every surface the university publishes, with a governance model that stops contradictions from returning.

In scope

  • All undergraduate, international, transfer, visiting, Medicine and preparatory-year admission pages.
  • The 11 graduate pages audited, plus two priced programmes that have no admission page.
  • Fees, payment and refund pages relevant to new applicants.
  • The public face of the application portal (welcome page, calendar and routing).
  • Linked PDF files and guides, and indexed legacy pages.
  • Arabic–English parity for every core component.

Out of scope

  • Changing admission policies themselves. The project records decisions; it does not make them.
  • Redesigning the whole university website beyond the admissions section.
  • Internal changes to the portal's logic after login, except intake dates and routing.
  • Student information systems (SIS) and internal billing.
  • Marketing campaigns and paid advertising.

Governance and decision rights

Governance · Decision Rights · RACI

The model is built on roles and functions. No personal names. D=decides · O=owns the data · A=approves publishing · P=publishes · V=verifies in production · M=monitors drift and expiry · C=consulted.

Data domainAdmissions Committee / Academic CouncilAdmissions DirectorFinanceCollegesEnglish ProgrammeIT / WebCorporate CommunicationsQuality
Academic formulas and thresholdsDO · A—CCPCV · M
English test thresholdsDA—COPCV · M
Major requirements (Medicine, Engineering, Computing, Architecture)DA—OCPCV · M
Fees, VAT and refundsCAD · OC—PCV · M
Application channels and datesCD · O—C—PCV · M
Required documentsCD · O—C—PCV
Arabic and English text—A———POV

The six governance answers

  • Who decides? The Admissions Committee or Academic Council for rules, Finance for money, the project sponsor for channels.
  • Who owns the data? One named functional owner for each of the 14 domains.
  • Who approves? The Admissions Director and the domain owner together, before any publishing.
  • Who publishes? The web team only, and only from the canonical record.
  • Who verifies in production? Quality or Institutional Effectiveness, independent of the publisher.
  • Who monitors drift and expiry? Content operations, with a monthly check and a check before each cycle opens.

Meeting structure

  • Steering committee (executive sponsor, Admissions Director, Finance, IT and Quality) every two weeks; decides at the closure gates.
  • Project team weekly, with the six workstream leads.
  • Escalation: any decision more than 5 working days late goes to the executive sponsor.

Method, coverage and limitations

Method · Coverage · Limitations

What was actually done

  • Discovery started from pmu.edu.sa/admission/admission and followed the navigation and sidebar links, then web search to find isolated pages.
  • Phase 1: the text of 22 pages was read in full through the accessibility tree in the user's browser (Opera), and 9 screenshots were taken. Phase 2: 48 more resources were read through a remote fetcher that renders the page after JavaScript runs (TinyFish), including every graduate, fees, refund and guide page.
  • Live verification (2026-09-27, 19:09–19:20 UTC): after pmu.edu.sa was allowed in the audit environment, 21 core surfaces were re-fetched by the G3 verifier (all HTTP 200) and measured with a read-only automated Chromium browser (Playwright) at two widths, 390×844 and 1366×768; 6 full-length mobile screenshots were taken (S30 to S35). No button was clicked and no form was filled.
  • Data points were extracted and compared across pages (data-point matrix), the published formulas were recomputed, and 9 external facts were verified against their official sources.
  • Every conflict was classified without choosing the correct value, unless a decisive official source existed.
  • Full review from the first step: 70 live resources were re-fetched and every quote was matched against them, the canonical record and decision packs were regenerated (byte-identical), the 15 legacy URLs were checked (14 are still published), and the external facts were re-checked through their official sites. Details in REVIEW-2026-09-27.md.

Declared limitations

  • In phases 1 and 2 the audit environment blocked pmu.edu.sa, so pages were read through the user's browser and a remote tool. The block was later lifted for that domain only: the 21 surfaces were fetched first, then 70 of the 72 resources were re-fetched in the full review (all HTTP 200). External sources stayed blocked, so they were re-checked by searching their official sites. Plain http:// requests are still refused by the network proxy, so nothing is claimed about how unencrypted links behave on the server.
  • Phase 1 screenshots (S01 to S22) show the top of the page only because scrolling was not possible; S30 to S35 are full length. Text quoted from lower on the page is documented as text, and every screenshot states exactly what it proves.
  • Phase 3 (connector sweep): the Aptis guide (August 2017) and the Student Handbook (May 2019) were opened, and legacy page metadata was checked: no noindex and no canonical. Remote Desktop Commander is connected to your computer, but this session's permission policy blocked running commands on it. Ahrefs is installed but not fully connected. The Opera browser was offline. Not yet opened: the payment-schedule tab content. The logged-in portal is outside the bounds of a read-only audit.
  • DOM measurements were taken on the English version of 21 surfaces only. The Arabic version, colour contrast, keyboard operation and screen-reader output were not tested, so no WCAG compliance is claimed.
  • No earlier report or findings were used. The portal admin tab open in the browser was not opened, and no personal data was recorded.

Self-correction and verified passes

Self-Correction · Verified Passes

Corrected or withdrawn before publication

  • Number of formulas: the first version said “five formulas for the same stage”. On re-reading: four weighting schemes, and the same-category conflict is three rules for the general high-school applicant. The wording was corrected; severity stays Critical.
  • The uap.sa link on the Medicine page: looked like an error at first, then it turned out the university's own calendar relies on the Qabool platform. Withdrawn as an error and reclassified as an internal routing conflict.
  • Programme catalogue: the first read showed “only 6 Business programmes” because of the extraction method; the full fetch showed the whole list. Withdrawn and replaced by the real gap: Medicine and three priced programmes are missing.
  • Arabic: Arabic was found on the apply page and the query form, so the wording was refined. The finding stands because Arabic is absent from every rule page.
