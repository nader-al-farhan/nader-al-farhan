International Admissions Office
PMU’s International Admissions Office offers a range of different programs for international
students. From choosing a program to applying to accept Admission Offer to issuing
student visa to housing to start the study, PMU’s International Admissions Office
staff will be there for you.
Apply
As an international student, you'll begin the application process just like all
other applicants. The application process can be completed entirely online.

Freshman Students
Applicants who have completed their secondary school and seek direct entry to the
undergraduate program, and wish to bypass the Preparatory Program, can apply to
PMU for freshman admissions. However, they have to provide a PMU recognized proficiency
English language test certificate with acceptable scores.
Admission Criteria
The applicant’s eligibility for direct admission into degree studies is based on
the following:
Completed online
application
Acceptable secondary school GPA *80% or equivalent
Acceptable SAT I
A valid IELTS certificate (Academic version) with Minimum scores of 6.0 overall
and 5.5 in writing
Personal interview with the Admissions Office (ONLINE)
*Students with a lower percentage can apply, however, acceptance will depend
on the overall results of the following criteria:
Non-Engineering Majors:
Secondary School 60%
SAT I 40%
Computer Science & Engineering Majors:
Secondary School 40%
SAT I 30%
SAT II 30%

Transfer Students
Students coming from other universities, who wish to transfer their credits to PMU,
may apply for transfer admissions. However, an applicant may be admitted to the
Preparatory Program or directly to the degree program, based on the applicant’s
English language proficiency. Transfer applicants should have a good academic standing
from their previous recognized university with the minimum PMU required grades and
cumulative GPA.
Admission Criteria
In order to be consider as a transfer candidate, the below criteria should be met
in addition to the
Admission requirements.
Eligibility:
Previous Institution is international university or can be a college/community college
in a Western country (Associate Degree or above)
Previous type of study should be regular and not online or distance learning
Previous institution must be recognized by Saudi Ministry of Education (MOE)
The total GPA is 2.0 out of 4.0 from the previous institution
The applicant should not be dismissed from the previous institution
The applicant must meet the current PMU admission criteria
Applicants should have achieved the minimum required grades and cumulative GPA (for
more information
(click here)
Last course passed on the submitted transcript should not be older than 5 years

Visitor Students
Visiting students are students registered in other international recognized universities
requesting to take courses at PMU that will count towards their degrees in their
home institutions. To be able to register for a course as a visiting student, the
applicant should meet the particular course requirements and pre-requisites, secure
an approval from the home institution, and be approved by the college offering the
course at PMU. All undergraduate courses at PMU are taught in English and the applicant
should also meet English language requirements to be admitted as a visiting student.
Visiting students are temporary members of the PMU community. They are expected
to observe all academic and non-academic regulations. Visiting students may choose
to transfer to PMU as resident students, in which case the transfer policy applies.
Admission Criteria
In order to be considered as a visiting candidate, the below criteria should be
met.
Eligibility:
Meet the particular course requirements and pre-requisites
Meet the English Language proficiency level of the courses
Secure an official approval from the home institution
The Course(s) should be offered and available for visiting students for the applied
semester
The visiting request should be approved by the college offering the course at PMU

Housing
Developing a residence community is an important step in the evolution of PMU as
a community of scholars. Because students who live together more readily collaborate
and support each other toward the goal of earning a degree, various academic support
elements will be located in the residence halls for the convenience of students.

Campus Life
The Campus Life Department offers a variety of programs and services that are intentionally
designed to complement and enhance the student’s educational experience at PMU.
