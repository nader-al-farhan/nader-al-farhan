For students admitted/readmitted from Fall Semester 2025/2026
Students are required to pay the full semester tuition fee in advance of the registration period. Any outstanding balance must be settled no later than July 10, 2026, to avoid the imposition of a late payment penalty.
For continuing students admitted before Fall Semester 2025/2026
Students are encouraged to settle the full semester tuition fee before the first day of classes. However, payment may also be made in accordance with the instalment schedule outlined below:
