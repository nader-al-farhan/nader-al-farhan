English Language Proficiency Tests
PMU requires a certain level of English language proficiency to join the programs. To be more objective in placing candidates on appropriate levels, PMU has used international English language proficiency tests for admissions that ensure the accuracy of the outcomes. Read more about the current technology used for the English placement test Aptis. Candidates with PMU recognized proficiency English language test certificates can verify the admission requirements on the following links IELTS requirements and TOEFL requirements.
