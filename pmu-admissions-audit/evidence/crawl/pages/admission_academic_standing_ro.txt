Academic Standing 
 Placement on Probation
 Students will be placed on academic probation at the end of any semester in which his/her cumulative GPA is below 2.0. Students on probation have one semester in which to achieve a non-cumulative GPA of 2.0 or higher. 
 If the student on probation achieved non cumulative GPA of 2.0 or higher and cumulative GPA less than two, he/she will be placed on a second probation.
 If in any subsequent semester the student achieved cumulative GPA of less than 2.00, then he/she will be dismissed from the University. However, at the discretion of the Dean of the College or that Chair of the Academic Department , the student maybe given another chance to improve his/her cumulative GPA.
 Actions involving academic probation and dismissal are entered on the student’s permanent record.
Removal of Probation and Dismissal
 Probation will be removed at the end of any semester in which a student attains a cumulative GPA of 2.00 or higher.
