 Tips for a Smoother Application Process
 Remember that you will not be eligible for the Financial Aid without a PMU acceptance notice, including an ID number.
 Read the application procedures carefully, and apply early 
 If you have questions, you can send an e-mail, call, or visit the Financial Aid Office. Financial Aid staff can give you accurate information. 
 DO NOT WAIT UNTIL THE DEADLINE TO APPLY.
 The last few days preceding the deadline are usually the busiest, this means a longer waiting time to submit the application and documents. Moreover, You need few days to prepare all the documents required and all complete documents must be submitted with the application. 
 Schedule an interview as early as possible to allow more time for further assessment if needed.
 THE INTERVIEW IS A MUST. 
 Inform the financial aid staff of any changes promptly and support the changes with the required documents.
 Keep a financial aid file consisting of a copy of your application and the original documents that were submitted to the Financial Aid Office as copies.
