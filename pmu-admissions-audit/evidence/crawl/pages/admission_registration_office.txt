 The Registrar’s Office at PMU welcomes you! 
Congratulations on making your rightful choice in becoming a student and a member of PMU community. We at the Registrar’s Office continuously strive to offer you quality and prompt services through our qualified and friendly staff. Faculty members along with Registrar’s staff will always work with & advise you on all your academic plans through your academic journey at PMU. 
We look forward to working with you and we wish you successful academic year and once again welcome from the entire family of PMU.
Dr. Omar J. El-Moussa
Vice President of Student Affairs
