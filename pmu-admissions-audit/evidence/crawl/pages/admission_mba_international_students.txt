 MBA 
 The Prince Mohammad Bin Fahd University’s (PMU) Masters in Business Administration is designed for professionals interested in developing and
 acquiring knowledge beyond their initial degree in order to improve their career prospects. Learn More...
 Admissions to the Master of Business Administration (MBA) program
 The Master of Business Administration (MBA) is a 36 credit-hour evening program that can be pursued on a full-time or part-time basis, thus allowing participants 
 full flexibility when integrating graduate studies into their demanding personal and professional lives. After fulfilling common MBA core requirements, participants
 can choose from six different concentrations: General Management, International Finance, Project Management, Entrepreneurship and Innovation, Supply Chain 
 Management, and Marketing. Admission into the MBA does not require an undergraduate business degree, but non-business majors may be given the opportunity to take 
 four business foundation courses before beginning their MBA coursework which can then be completed in as few as two years. The program intends to empower 
 participants to successfully launch their own enterprises or to excel in middle and upper management positions at companies of all sizes and in all industries. 
 Admission Criteria 
 Educational Background: 
 A Bachelor’s Degree from a recognized university
 A minimum GPA of 2.50 out of 4.00 or equivalent (Very Good) in Bachelor’s Degree
 English Language Proficiency Test: 
 A valid IELTS certificate (Academic version) with minimum scores of 6.0 overall and 5.5 in each skill or equivalent TOEFL iBT.
 Personal Interview:
 Candidates will be personally interviewed by committee of program administrators and faculty
 Application Form 
 How to Apply:
 Complete online application 
 Submit documents before due date 
 Two recent passport-size photographs 
 Copy of Graduation’s Certificate certified by the Saudi Cultural Mission and Saudi Embassy
 Copy of Transcript
 Copy of National ID
 Copy of passport
 Pay a non-refundable application fee of SAR 950 (incl. VAT) upon submission of documents
 Submit original IELTS (Academic version) certificate with minimum scores of 6.0 overall and 5.5 in each skill or equivalent TOEFL iBT.
 Other requirements might be requested by the admissions office.Documents can be submitted online
 Admission Process
 After receiving and reviewing the above requirements, the admissions office will set an appointment for an interview 
 The final decision will be communicated through the Admissions office
 PMU will support in issuing student visa.
