 SCHOLASTIC APTITUDE TEST
 Scholastic Aptitude Test (SAT)
 PMU sets general requirements for candidates to apply. However, some majors require specific level of certain skills to apply for these specializations. These majors are Architecture, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering and Civil Engineering etc. Students with international high school certificate might submit SAT with minimum score of 1200 instead of Qudrat and Tahseely tests. 
 Applicants who scored 400 or more in SAT Math section may wave one or more of the mathematics courses in the Preparatory Program when they successfully meet one of the below categories:
