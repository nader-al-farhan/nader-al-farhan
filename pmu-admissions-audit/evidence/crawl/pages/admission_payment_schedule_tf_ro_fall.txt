 Payment Schedule FALL & Payment Method 
For Students Admitted/Readmitted from Fall Semester 2025/2026 onward
Students admitted or readmitted from Fall Semester 2025/2026 are required to pay the full required advance tuition fee of the semester based on their registered program/major before course registration.
For the applicable tuition fees, please refer to: Undergraduate Fees / Graduate Fees
