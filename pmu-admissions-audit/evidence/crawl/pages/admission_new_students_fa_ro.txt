 New Students 
 These are new students admitted to PMU and applying for financial aid, interested students are kindly asked to download, complete, and then submit the application IN PERSON along with all the required documents to the Financial Aid Office. 
 Students may as well obtain the application from the Financial Aid Office. It is very important to read instructions carefully before filling the application. 
 Applications that have missing information and/or missing documents will be considered incomplete and hence will not be evaluated. 
 Application forms are available at the Financial Aid Office.
 Applicants should consider not waiting till the last few days since filling the application and preparing all the required documents may take some time. During the last few days students may have to wait in queue lines.
