 Application Form 
 How to Apply:
 Complete online application 
 Submit documents before due date 
 Recent passport-size photographs 
 Copy of Graduation’s Certificate (MoE Equivalency letter if graduated abroad)
 Copy of Transcript
 Copy of Saudi ID 
 Copy of passport
 If employed, a letter of no objection from the employer is required
 Two reference letters from academics/professionals who can provide appropriate references
 Pay a non-refundable application fee of SAR 950 (incl. VAT) upon submission of documents
 A valid IELTS certificate (Academic version) with minimum scores of 6.0 overall and 5.5 in each skill or equivalent TOEFL iBT minimum scores of 83 overall and 19 in each skill (online or home edition is not accepted)
 Other requirements might be requested by the admissions office
 Documents should be uploaded online after receiving the application confirmation email, instructions will be included.
 Admission Process
 After receiving and reviewing the above requirements, the admissions office will set an appointment for an interview 
 The final decision will be communicated through the Admissions office
