 MSHD 
 The Master of Science in Education with its 42 credit hours is designed to advance the development of the necessary skills and knowledge of its candidates and help 
 them address and assess the challenges they face as educators in the 21st century.
 Admissions to the Master of Science in Human Development (MSHD) program
 Admissions Requirements 
 Educational Background
 A completed baccalaureate degree (with GPA 2.50/4.00) from an accredited and/or recognized university provided that the applicant’s field of specialization is relevant to a Master of Science in Human Development Degree.
 Official transcripts of all college work at previous institutions must be provided. 
 Work Experience
 Personal Data Sheet/Curriculum Vita: The Personal Data Sheet should include relevant biographical information and professional/educational experience. 
 English Language Proficiency Test
 A valid IELTS certificate (Academic version) with minimum scores of 6.0 overall and 5.5 in each skill or equivalent TOEFL iBT.
 Statement of Purpose
 A Statement of Purpose (usually around 500 words) in which the applicant summarizes his/her professional goals, including professional objectives, research interests, his/her motivation
 for graduate work and other significant information
 Letters of Reference
 Applicants must submit at least two letters of recommendation, which attest to their professional competence, academic preparation, and potential for graduate work. The two letters should clearly reflect professional success and foresee academic success at the graduate level.
 Ideally, one letter should be from a college or university professor who can discuss the Applicant’s academic abilities and one should be from an administrator or colleague who 
 can authoritatively describe the applicant’s professional experience and potential. 
 Personal Interview
 Each candidate who otherwise meets the selection criteria will be personally interviewed by a committee of program administrators and faculty. The interview will provide an opportunity to see the candidate in a setting of personal interaction and thus can help to 
 determine whether or not the candidate has the right skill set for the program. 
 Application Form 
 Required Documents
 Complete online application 
 Submit documents before due date 
 Two recent passport-size photographs 
 Copy of Graduation’s Certificate certified by the Saudi Cultural Mission and Saudi Embassy
 Copy of Transcript
 Personal resume in English
 Statement of motivation for the master degree (around 500 words)
 Copy of National ID
 Copy of passport
 Two recommendation letters in English, written and signed by persons who are well acquainted with the applicant’s work and studies and are in the position to judge the candidate’s abilities to pursue a Master’s Degree
 Pay a non-refundable application fee of SAR 950 (incl. VAT) upon submission of documents
 Submit original IELTS (Academic version) certificate with minimum scores of 6.0 overall and 5.5 in each skill or equivalent TOEFL iBT.
 Other requirements might be requested by the admissions office
 Documents can be submitted online 
 Admission Process
 After receiving and reviewing the above requirements, the admissions office will set an appointment for an interview 
 The final decision will be communicated through the Admissions office
 PMU will support in issuing student visa.
