 Admissions from Other Universities 
 Transfer Admissions
 Students coming from other universities, who wish to transfer their credits to PMU, may apply for transfer admissions. However, an applicant may be admitted to the Preparatory Program or directly to the degree program, based on the applicant’s English language proficiency. Transfer applicants should have a good academic standing from their previous recognized university with the minimum PMU required grades and cumulative GPA.
 Admission Criteria 
 In order to be consider as a transfer candidate, the below criteria should be met in addition to the Admission requirements.
 Eligibility:
 Previous Institution is a local government university, local private university, international university or can be a college/community college in a Western country (Associate Degree or above)
 Previous type of study should be regular and not online or distance learning
 Previous institution must be recognized by Ministry of Education (MOE)
 The total GPA is 2.0 out of 4.0 from the previous institution
 The applicant should not be dismissed from the previous institution
 The applicant must meet the current PMU admission criteria
 Applicants should have achieved the minimum required grades and cumulative GPA (for more information click here)
 Last course passed on the submitted transcript should not be older than 5 years
 How to Apply:
 Complete online application
 Submit documents before due date
 Two recent passport-size photographs
 Copy of secondary school certificate or equivalent
 Copy of General Aptitude Test score report (Qudrat) or equivalent (SAT 1)
 Copy of Saudi ID or “Iqama” (for Non-Saudis)
 Copy of Family ID Card (For female applicants - if available)
 Copy of passport
 If employed, a letter of no objection from the employer is required
 Original sealed transcript (English version)
 A copy of all courses syllabi (English version)
 Pay a non-refundable application fee of SAR 950 (incl. VAT) upon submission of documents
 For direct entry, submit original IELTS (Academic version) certificate with minimum scores of 6.0 overall and 5.5 in writing or equivalent TOEFL iBT with acceptable scores (read more...)
 Admission Process
 After receiving and reviewing the above requirements, the admissions office will set an appointment for English placement test (Aptis Placement Test) and interview 
 Upon acceptance, the transfer committee will review the credit transfer request (minimum 5 weeks)
 The final decision will be communicated through the Admissions Office
