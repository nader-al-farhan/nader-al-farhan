 Undergraduate Fees
 Undergraduate Fees (For students admitted/readmitted from Fall Semester 2025/2026) 
 Full-Time Students
 Tuition Fee – All Programs
 SAR 29,000 / semester: Covers enrollment of 12 to 18 credit/billable hours.
 Additional hours (19 or more): Each credit/billable hour beyond 18 will be charged at SAR 2,416.
 Tuition Fee – Artificial Intelligence & Cybersecurity Majors
 SAR 32,500 / semester: Covers enrollment of 12 to 18 credit/billable hours.
 Additional hours (19 or more): Each credit/billable hour beyond 18 will be charged at SAR 2,708.3.
 Part-Time Students
 Tuition Fee – All Programs
 SAR 2,416.7 per credit/billable hour: Applies to students enrolled in 11 credit/billable hours or less.
 Tuition Fee – Artificial Intelligence & Cybersecurity Majors
 SAR 2,708.3 per credit/billable hour: Applies to students enrolled in 11 credit/billable hours or less.
 Value Added Tax (VAT)
 VAT for Non-Saudi Students
 A 15% VAT applies to tuition and fees.
 VAT for Saudi Students
 A 15% VAT applies to fees only. Saudi students do not pay VAT on tuition.
